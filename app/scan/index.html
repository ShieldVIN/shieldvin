What a green tick does — and does not — mean

Every verdict here is backed by a claim on a public ledger: a zero-knowledge proof was made against the anchored record, and the proof could not have been produced if the claim were false of that record. What the proof cannot tell you is whether the record matched physical reality when it was written — the registrar is trusted for that, which is why every passport names one.

So a tick means proven against the anchored record, attributable, and tamper-evident — not that fraud is impossible. And "not proven" means exactly that: no claim exists. It is not a "no".
